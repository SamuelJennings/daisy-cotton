Positioning uses the popover method: the trigger's `popovertarget` opens the panel, and CSS anchor positioning (`anchor-name` on the trigger, `position-anchor` on the panel) places it. A browser without anchor positioning support still opens and closes the panel, but it may not sit beside its trigger — that is a limit of the method, not a bug here.
